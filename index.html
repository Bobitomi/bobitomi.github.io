Shift handovers

Relay helps an outgoing crew pass open work to the next shift. The receiving team can see what happened, who owns each follow-up, and what is due next.

Inside the workspace

Teams log incidents, assign actions, and prepare a dated handover. The incoming leader acknowledges the handover. Open issues keep their owners and history as they move between shifts.

Why Relay asks for Gmail access

The operator connects one sending mailbox for account verification, team invitations, password resets and security notices, handover reminders, and operational emails requested by authorized crew members. Only the sender authorizes Gmail access; crew members use their own Relay accounts and receive messages without connecting their Gmail accounts.

A crew member can preview a record email, such as an incident or KYC case, and choose its recipients before sending. The company leader controls permission to send outside the company. Relay requests only gmail.send; it cannot read a Gmail inbox or contacts with this permission.
