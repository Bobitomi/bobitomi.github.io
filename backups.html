Relay Backups

A private backup connection for the operator of Relay, the shift-handover workspace.

What it does

Relay creates encrypted copies of its server database. With the Drive owner's approval, Relay Backups copies those files to a private Google Drive folder and checks their size and checksum. The operator can download a copy for an isolated recovery test.

Why Google Drive access is needed

The connection uses only drive.file: permission to see, edit, create and delete the specific Drive files used with this app. The backup process creates a private folder, lists its backup files, uploads missing encrypted copies and verifies the results. It does not request access to the owner's entire Drive, Gmail or contacts.

The Drive owner connects the service once. Crew members do not connect their Google accounts. When configured on Relay's server, backups can run while the operator's computer is off.

Keeping control

Drive stores encrypted backup files. The decryption key is kept separately. Backups remain private to the connected Drive owner; they are not published on this website. The owner can disconnect the app through Google Account connections.

Read the Relay Backups privacy notice for data, storage and deletion details. For support, contact the Relay operator using the support email shown on Google's consent screen.
