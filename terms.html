Trial terms

Relay Trial is a workspace for evaluating shift handovers, issues, and follow-up actions with your company’s crew.

Accounts and access

Use your own Relay account and the teams your company assigns to you. Keep invitations and password-reset links private. Contact your company leader if you receive an unexpected invitation or gain access to records outside your assignment.

Recording shift work

Enter information you are authorized to share with the team. Use your company’s procedures for urgent incidents and escalations. The receiving crew is responsible for reviewing the handover and following up on open actions.

Email and support

Only a mailbox owner or authorized operator may connect a sender. Use the connection for trial account emails and requested delivery checks. Report an access or delivery problem to your company leader or the support address on the Google consent screen. Read the privacy policy for details about sender authorization and stored records.
